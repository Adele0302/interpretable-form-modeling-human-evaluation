# Privacy and Data-Release Policy

This repository follows a **data-minimization** approach for human-subject research artifacts.

## Publicly released

- audited sample-count and representation metadata;
- PCA explained-variance summaries;
- aggregate human-rating QC counts;
- final leakage-aware model-performance summaries;
- aggregate 2AFC comparison statistics;
- condition-level interaction-study statistics;
- methods documentation and verification scripts.

## Not publicly released

- participant-level behavioral logs from the 18-person interaction study;
- individual decision trajectories;
- raw individual 2AFC response rows;
- raw questionnaire exports;
- real session identifiers and collected event histories;
- names, contact information, IP addresses, signatures, or consent records;
- private mapping keys;
- SQLite databases created during data collection;
- original source images whose redistribution rights are not established;
- complete contour/width matrices in this lightweight repository;
- synthetic/test participant data presented as if they were empirical findings.

The absence of participant-level data is intentional. Transparency is provided through methods, audited study metadata, aggregate statistical outputs, and executable verification checks without unnecessarily disclosing human-subject records.
